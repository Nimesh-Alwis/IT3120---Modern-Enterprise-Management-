## 3. ES / EF / LS / LF

මේවා Activity එකක් පටන් ගන්න සහ ඉවර කරන්න පුළුවන් කාලයන්.

**ES – Early Start:** Activity එක පටන් ගන්න පුළුවන් මුල්ම වෙලාව.

**EF – Early Finish:** Activity එක ඉවර කරන්න පුළුවන් මුල්ම වෙලාව.

**LS – Late Start:** Project එක Late නොකර Activity එක පටන් ගන්න පුළුවන් අවසාන වෙලාව.

**LF – Late Finish:** Project එක Late නොකර Activity එක ඉවර කරන්න පුළුවන් අවසාන වෙලාව.

## 4. Earned Value Management (EVM)

**Earned Value Management (EVM)** කියන්නේ Project එකේ Schedule Performance සහ Cost Performance මැනීමට භාවිතා කරන ක්‍රමයකි.

### Key Components

**Planned Value (PV):** සැලසුම් කර ඇති වැඩ සඳහා වෙන් කළ Budgeted Value එක.

**Earned Value (EV):** මේ වන විට සම්පූර්ණ කර ඇති වැඩවල Value එක.

**Actual Cost (AC):** සම්පූර්ණ කර ඇති වැඩ සඳහා සැබෑවටම වැය වූ මුදල.

### Important EVM Metrics

#### 1. Schedule Variance (SV)

Project එකේ වැඩ Schedule එකට සාපේක්ෂව ඉදිරියෙන්ද, පසුපසින්ද කියා මැනීමට භාවිතා කරයි.

> **SV = EV − PV**

**SV = 0:** Project එක Schedule එකට අනුව සිදු වේ (**On Schedule**).

**SV > 0:** Project එක Schedule එකට වඩා ඉදිරියෙන් ඇත (**Ahead of Schedule**).

**SV < 0:** Project එක Schedule එකට වඩා පසුපසින් ඇත (**Behind Schedule**).

#### 2. Schedule Performance Index (SPI)

Project එකේ Schedule Efficiency එක මැනීමට භාවිතා කරයි.

> **SPI = EV / PV**

**SPI = 1:** Project එක Schedule එකට අනුව සිදු වේ (**On Schedule**).

**SPI > 1:** Project එක Schedule එකට වඩා ඉදිරියෙන් ඇත (**Ahead of Schedule**).

**SPI < 1:** Project එක Schedule එකට වඩා පසුපසින් ඇත (**Behind Schedule**).

#### 3. Cost Variance (CV)

Project එකේ වියදම් Budget එකට සාපේක්ෂව පාලනය වී තිබේද කියා මැනීමට භාවිතා කරයි.

> **CV = EV − AC**

**CV = 0:** Project එක Budget එකට අනුව සිදු වේ (**On Budget**).

**CV > 0:** සැලසුම් කළ මුදලට වඩා අඩුවෙන් වැය වී ඇත (**Under Budget**).

**CV < 0:** සැලසුම් කළ මුදලට වඩා වැඩියෙන් වැය වී ඇත (**Over Budget**).

#### 4. Cost Performance Index (CPI)

Project එකේ Cost Efficiency එක මැනීමට භාවිතා කරයි.

> **CPI = EV / AC**

**CPI = 1:** Project එක Budget එකට අනුව සිදු වේ (**On Budget**).

**CPI > 1:** Project එකේ වියදම් කාර්යක්ෂමයි (**Cost Efficient / Under Budget**).

**CPI < 1:** Project එකේ වියදම Budget එක ඉක්මවා ඇත (**Cost Overrun / Over Budget**).

### Quick Summary

**SV:** `EV − PV` — Schedule Performance මැනීම.

**SPI:** `EV / PV` — Schedule Efficiency මැනීම.

**CV:** `EV − AC` — Cost Performance මැනීම.

**CPI:** `EV / AC` — Cost Efficiency මැනීම.

## 5. Activity Dependency Types

Activities දෙකක් අතර සම්බන්ධතාවය දැක්වීමට පහත Dependency Types භාවිතා කරයි. මෙහි **A** යනු පෙර Activity එක වන අතර **B** යනු එය මත රඳා පවතින Activity එකයි.

**Finish-to-Start (FS):** **A** ඉවර වෙනකම් **B** පටන් ගන්න බැහැ.

**Start-to-Start (SS):** **A** පටන් ගන්නකම් **B** පටන් ගන්න බැහැ.

**Finish-to-Finish (FF):** **A** ඉවර වෙනකම් **B** ඉවර කරන්න බැහැ.

**Start-to-Finish (SF):** **A** පටන් ගන්නකම් **B** ඉවර කරන්න බැහැ.

### Quick Reference

**FS:** Activity **A** Finish වීමෙන් පසුව Activity **B** Start කළ හැකියි.

**SS:** Activity **A** Start වීමෙන් පසුව Activity **B** Start කළ හැකියි.

**FF:** Activity **A** Finish වීමෙන් පසුව Activity **B** Finish කළ හැකියි.

**SF:** Activity **A** Start වීමෙන් පසුව Activity **B** Finish කළ හැකියි.

## 6. Scope and Integration Management – 3 Marks

### Question

The university requests an AI-based chatbot after the project scope has been approved. Explain three actions the project manager should take to evaluate and manage this change, considering its effects on scope, schedule, cost, and integration with existing modules.

### Answer

#### Assess the Change Impact

Evaluate how the AI chatbot affects the project scope, schedule, cost, required resources, and integration with existing modules.

#### Follow the Change Control Process

Submit a formal change request and obtain approval from the Change Control Board (CCB) or authorized stakeholders before implementation.

#### Update Project Plans and Integrate

After approval, update the scope, schedule, and cost baselines. Then plan, develop, and test the chatbot's integration with the existing system modules.

## 7. Opportunity and Feasibility – 4 Marks

### Question

Evaluate the technical, market, operational, and financial feasibility of the proposed application.

### Answer

#### Technical Feasibility

The application can be developed using existing technologies. However, developing all the proposed features may be difficult with limited resources.

#### Market Feasibility

There is a potential market among farmers and buyers. However, existing supplier relationships and low digital literacy may reduce adoption of the application.

#### Operational Feasibility

The platform can improve buying and selling activities. However, unreliable delivery arrangements and limited digital skills may affect its daily operations.

#### Financial Feasibility

The startup has **Rs. 2 million**, while full development requires **Rs. 3.5 million**. Therefore, there is a funding gap of **Rs. 1.5 million**. Developing a **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** is a suitable option within the available budget.

## 8. Lean Startup and MVP – 4 Marks

### Question

Explain Lean Startup, propose three MVP features, and explain how customer feedback informs future development.

### Answer in Simple English

#### Lean Startup

Lean Startup is an approach that develops a basic product, tests it with real customers, collects feedback, and improves it using the **Build–Measure–Learn** cycle.

#### Three MVP Features for AgriConnect Lanka

##### Product Listings

Farmers can list their agricultural products with prices and available quantities.

##### Product Search

Buyers can search for available agricultural products.

##### Basic Order Management

Buyers can place orders, and farmers can view and manage those orders.

#### How Feedback Informs Development

Test the MVP with farmers and buyers in one district. Collect their feedback about usability, pricing, and ordering problems. Use this feedback to improve the application and decide which features should be developed next.

## 9. Startup Risks – 4 Marks

### Question

Identify four significant startup risks and provide one mitigation measure for each risk.

### Answer in Simple English

#### Financial Risk

**Mitigation Measure:** Start with a low-cost MVP to reduce development costs.

#### Market Risk

**Mitigation Measure:** Conduct market research and pilot testing to understand customer needs and attract buyers.

#### Operational Risk

**Mitigation Measure:** Partner with reliable delivery providers to ensure timely deliveries.

#### Technology Adoption Risk

**Mitigation Measure:** Provide a simple application interface and training for farmers.

## 10. Business Model and Growth – 3 Marks

### Question

Recommend an initial target market, a value proposition, and one scaling strategy for the business.

### Answer in Simple English

#### Initial Target Market

Target small-scale farmers and local restaurants in one district to test the platform.

#### Value Proposition

Provide an easy-to-use digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with buyers, offers transparent prices, and makes buying and selling easier.

#### Scaling Strategy

After a successful pilot, expand the platform to other districts based on customer feedback and market demand.
